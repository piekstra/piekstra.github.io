mychart: Terms of Use

Effective 16 November 2026. These terms apply when you link mychart to your MyChart account at a health system.

What mychart is

mychart is free, open-source software that runs on your own computer. It signs in to MyChart through your health system and downloads the health records your MyChart account lets you share with apps: your own, and those of people whose records you can see through MyChart proxy access. Its source code is public at github.com/own-your-health/mychart-cli.

Who may use it

Use mychart only for your own records, and for records you are allowed to see through MyChart proxy access. Each copy of mychart is registered with Epic by the person or household that uses it.

What it does

mychart only reads. It never writes, changes or deletes anything at your health system. It saves records only on the computer it runs on, where you tell it to. It sends none of your records, and nothing about you, to its developers or to anyone else. The privacy page says what it keeps and where.

Your part

The records mychart saves are your health information. Keep the computer and the saved files secure, and delete them when you no longer need them.

Not medical advice

mychart passes on records as your health system provides them, and they may be incomplete or out of date. It does not interpret them. Ask your care team about your health.

Ending access

mychart auth logout deletes the sign-in stored on your computer. In MyChart, "Manage My Linked Apps and Devices" ends mychart's access at your health system. Access also ends by itself after the period you chose when you signed in.
